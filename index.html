      Frequently asked

      Is a Hackintosh legal?

      Apple's macOS licence only allows installing macOS on Apple-branded hardware, so running it on other machines breaks the licence terms. It's widely done for learning and hobby use, but it isn't something to rely on for commercial work.

      Which macOS version should I install?

      The newest version your laptop is known to support, unless you depend on hardware it dropped. For example, macOS 14 Sonoma removed drivers for older Broadcom Wi-Fi cards, which then need extra patches. Check the laptop's page first.

      Can I use an AMD Ryzen or 11th gen+ Intel laptop?

      Not well. Intel 11th gen and newer use Iris Xe graphics, which has no macOS acceleration. AMD laptop graphics need experimental patches. Stick to Intel 8th–10th gen for a good experience.

      Will macOS updates break my install?

      Minor updates usually install fine once your kexts are current. Major releases often need updated OpenCore and kexts. Back up your EFI and wait for community reports before upgrading.

      Do iMessage and iCloud work?

      Yes, with a valid, unique SMBIOS serial and a correctly set-up network interface. See the Apple services section of the Troubleshooting page.

      What does “Unverified” mean on a laptop?

      Those values come from typical configurations or community lists and haven't been confirmed against a working build. Treat them as a starting point and check them for your exact model.
