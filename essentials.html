The Essentials

Beyond hardware, a successful build needs the right software, a bit of patience, and good sources. Here's the short list.

The build at a glance

  1. 1

    Research & planning

    Identify your exact CPU, Wi-Fi card, audio codec and trackpad. Read the Dortania guide for your CPU generation.

  2. 2

    BIOS & USB installer

    Configure the BIOS, download macOS, and build an OpenCore EFI on a USB stick.

  3. 3

    Installation

    Boot the USB and install macOS. Expect to troubleshoot a hang or two.

  4. 4

    Post-install tuning

    Copy the EFI to the internal drive, then fix Wi-Fi, audio, USB mapping, sleep and battery reporting.

Mindset

Hackintoshing is a rewarding hobby, not a replacement for a real Mac if you need everything to work flawlessly out of the box. Budget a weekend for the first build.

Back up before every update. macOS updates can break kexts or patches. Keep a copy of your working EFI on a spare USB stick, and wait a week or two after a release for the community to report problems.

macOS 26 Tahoe is the last macOS release for Intel Macs, which means it's the last one for Hackintoshes too.

Toolbox

  • OpenCore ↗
    The bootloader that makes it all work.
  • ProperTree ↗
    Cross-platform editor for config.plist, with OC Snapshot.
  • SSDTTime ↗
    Generates the ACPI patches (SSDTs) your laptop needs.
  • USBToolBox ↗
    Maps USB ports from Windows so you stay under macOS's 15-port limit.
  • GenSMBIOS ↗
    Creates serial numbers for your SMBIOS.
  • OpenCore Legacy Patcher ↗
    Root patches for hardware newer macOS dropped, such as Broadcom Wi-Fi on Sonoma and later.

Core kexts for laptops

Lilu:
patching framework most other kexts depend on.
VirtualSMC:
emulates the Mac SMC; add SMCBatteryManager for battery status.
WhateverGreen:
Intel iGPU and display fixes.
AppleALC:
onboard audio, configured with a layout-id.
VoodooPS2Controller:
PS/2 keyboards and trackpads.
VoodooI2C / VoodooRMI:
I2C and Synaptics SMBus trackpads with gestures.
itlwm / AirportItlwm:
Intel Wi-Fi support from OpenIntelWireless.
NVMeFix:
power management for third-party NVMe drives.
